聖城幣塔（Crusader Coin Pusher）隱私權政策

生效日期： 2026 年 9 月 29 日 適用範圍： Android 版 App 與網頁版 營運者： JohnWu

我們寫這份政策的目的，是讓你用最短的時間搞懂：玩《聖城幣塔》時，我們會拿到你的哪些資料、放在哪裡、給誰、放多久、你可以怎麼處理。

一句話總結

  • 你不登入也能玩，遊戲進度只存在你自己的裝置上。 - 如果你選擇登入（Google 帳號或 Email），我們只會拿到帳號識別資訊（一組帳號 ID、你的 Email），用來讓你的遊戲進度可以跨裝置同步。 - 遊戲沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤、沒有崩潰回報 SDK、沒有內購。 - 我們不會讀取你的位置、聯絡人、相機、麥克風、相簿、電話號碼或裝置識別碼。

1. 我們會蒐集哪些資料，以及用途

1.1 你不登入時（本機遊玩）

資料用途存放位置
遊戲進度（盤面、金幣數、塔、試煉、獎盃）讓你下次打開能接著玩只在你的裝置上（瀏覽器／App 的 IndexedDB 與 localStorage）
音效偏好（靜音、金幣音效設定）記住你的設定只在你的裝置上

這些資料不會上傳到任何伺服器。

1.2 你選擇登入時（雲端存檔）

登入是選擇性的。登入後我們會額外處理：

資料用途存放位置
帳號 ID（由 Google Firebase 產生的一組隨機識別碼）辨識你的帳號、決定雲端存檔存在哪個路徑、確保只有你本人能讀寫自己的存檔Google Firebase（雲端）與你的裝置
Email 地址（Email 登入時由你輸入；Google 登入時來自你的 Google 帳號）登入與註冊；登入後在「帳號」面板顯示，讓你知道目前登入的是哪個帳號由 Google Firebase Authentication 保存；App 本身不會把 Email 寫進遊戲存檔或雲端資料庫
密碼（僅 Email 登入）驗證身分App 不儲存密碼，只在登入當下交給 Google Firebase Authentication 處理
Google 登入憑證（一次性 ID token）用 Google 帳號登入只在記憶體中短暫存在，不會寫入硬碟或記錄在日誌中
遊戲進度（同 1.1）跨裝置同步、登出前備份Google Cloud Firestore（雲端）與你的裝置
存檔時間戳判斷哪一份存檔比較新隨存檔一起存放
遊玩工作階段標記（每次開啟 App 隨機產生的一組 UUID）避免兩台裝置同時對同一份存檔寫入；它不是裝置識別碼，每次開啟都不一樣Google Cloud Firestore 與你的裝置

我們不會讀取或儲存你 Google 帳號的顯示名稱、大頭貼或電話號碼。 也可以用「訪客」方式登入：這會產生一組匿名帳號 ID，但不需要 Email。

1.3 雲端同步的時機

登入後，遊戲進度會在以下時機上傳到雲端：啟動 App 時、每 10 分鐘一次、回到前景或網路恢復時、以及離開或登出時。

1.4 我們不蒐集的資料

  • 位置資訊、聯絡人、相機、麥克風、相片或檔案 - 裝置識別碼（Android ID、廣告 ID、IMEI 等） - 使用行為分析、崩潰報告、診斷資料 - 付款或財務資訊（遊戲內金幣是虛擬數值，沒有內購）

2. 資料存放在哪裡、服務供應商

我們使用 Google Firebase（Google LLC 提供）作為唯一的雲端服務供應商：

  • Firebase Authentication：處理登入（Google 帳號、Email／密碼、匿名訪客）。 - Cloud Firestore：存放你的雲端遊戲存檔。

雲端存檔的存取規則設定為「只有帳號本人能讀寫自己的資料」。 Google 在提供 Firebase 服務時，會依其隱私政策於伺服器端處理連線資訊（例如 IP 位址與瀏覽器／裝置的 user-agent 字串）以防止濫用。這是服務供應商層級的處理，App 本身不會讀取或儲存這些資訊。

  • Google 隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase 隱私與安全說明：https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Android 裝置備份：Android 系統可能會依你的裝置設定，將 App 的本機資料（包含本機存檔與登入狀態）備份到你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。這是 Android 系統功能，由你在裝置的「備份」設定中控制。

3. 資料分享

  • 我們不會把你的資料賣給、租給或分享給任何廣告商、分析公司或其他第三方。
  • App 內沒有任何廣告 SDK、分析 SDK 或崩潰回報 SDK。 - App 對外的唯一網路連線就是 Google Firebase。 - 唯一的例外是第 2 節所述的服務供應商 Google Firebase，它只為了提供登入與雲端存檔服務而處理你的資料。

4. 保存期限

  • 本機資料：一直存在你的裝置上，直到你解除安裝 App、清除 App 資料或清除瀏覽器站台資料。登出不會刪除本機存檔與備份。 - 雲端存檔與帳號：會一直保存到你請求刪除為止（見第 5 節）。 - 密碼與 Google 登入憑證：App 不保存。 - 遊玩工作階段標記：每次開啟 App 都會換新，舊的在 120 秒沒續約後即失效。

5. 你的權利：查看、刪除帳號與存檔

查看：登入後，在遊戲的「帳號」面板可以看到目前登入的 Email 與帳號 ID。 登出：在「帳號」面板點「登出」。登出會先把進度備份到本機並同步到雲端，然後結束登入狀態。 刪除帳號與雲端存檔：目前 App 內尚未提供刪除帳號的功能。 如果你想刪除帳號、雲端存檔或任何與你帳號相關的資料，請寄信到 johnwu0626@gmail.com，並用你登入時的 Email 寄出（或在信中附上「帳號」面板顯示的帳號 ID），我們會在 30 天內完成刪除並回覆你。刪除後，雲端存檔無法復原。

刪除本機資料：解除安裝 App，或在瀏覽器中清除本站的站台資料即可。

6. 兒童

本遊戲並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們也不會刻意蒐集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資料。若你認為有兒童未經同意向我們提供了資料，請透過第 9 節的方式聯絡我們，我們會將其刪除。

7. 傳輸與儲存安全

  • App 與 Google Firebase 之間的所有連線都經由 HTTPS／TLS 加密。 - Android 版不允許未加密的明文網路連線。 - 密碼由 Google Firebase Authentication 保管，App 本身不會儲存或記錄。 - 雲端存檔以帳號本人為唯一授權對象。

8. 政策更新

我們若修改這份政策，會更新頁首的生效日期，並在 https://johnwu0626.github.io/crusader-coin-pusher-demo/privacy/ 公告最新版本。若變更會實質影響你的資料被處理的方式，我們會在 App 內或以你登入的 Email 通知你。

Crusader Coin Pusher (聖城幣塔) Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 Applies to: the Android app and the web version Operator: JohnWu

This policy is written so you can understand, in a few minutes, what data we get from you when you play Crusader Coin Pusher, where it lives, who sees it, how long it stays, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You can play without signing in. Your progress then stays only on your own device. - If you choose to sign in (Google account or email), we only receive account identification data (an account ID and your email) so your progress can sync across devices. - The game has no ads, no analytics tracking, no crash-reporting SDK, and no in-app purchases. - We do not read your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, phone number, or device identifiers.

1. What we collect and why

1.1 When you do not sign in (local play)

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Game progress (board, coins, towers, trials, trophies)So you can continue next timeOnly on your device (the browser's / app's IndexedDB and localStorage)
Audio preferences (mute, coin sound settings)To remember your settingsOnly on your device

This data is never uploaded to any server.

1.2 When you choose to sign in (cloud saves)

Signing in is optional. When you do, we additionally process:

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Account ID (a random identifier generated by Google Firebase)Identify your account, decide where your cloud save lives, and make sure only you can read or write itGoogle Firebase (cloud) and your device
Email address (typed by you for email sign-in; from your Google account for Google sign-in)Sign-in and registration; shown in the Account panel so you know which account is activeKept by Google Firebase Authentication; the app itself never writes your email into game saves or the cloud database
Password (email sign-in only)Verify your identityNot stored by the app; passed to Google Firebase Authentication only at the moment you sign in
Google sign-in credential (a one-time ID token)Sign in with your Google accountHeld briefly in memory only; never written to disk or logged
Game progress (as in 1.1)Cross-device sync and a backup before sign-outGoogle Cloud Firestore (cloud) and your device
Save timestampsDecide which save is newerStored together with the save
Play-session marker (a random UUID created each time the app opens)Prevent two devices from writing the same save at once; it is not a device identifier and changes every launchGoogle Cloud Firestore and your device

We do not read or store your Google account's display name, profile photo, or phone number. You can also sign in as a guest: this creates an anonymous account ID without an email.

1.3 When cloud sync happens

After sign-in, your progress is uploaded when the app starts, every 10 minutes, when the app returns to the foreground or the network comes back, and when you leave or sign out.

1.4 What we do not collect

  • Location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files - Device identifiers (Android ID, advertising ID, IMEI, etc.) - Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics - Payment or financial information (in-game coins are virtual; there are no in-app purchases)

2. Where data is stored and our service provider

We use Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC) as our only cloud service provider:

  • Firebase Authentication handles sign-in (Google account, email/password, anonymous guest). - Cloud Firestore stores your cloud game saves.

Cloud saves are protected by access rules so that only the account owner can read or write their own data. When providing Firebase, Google processes connection information on its servers (such as IP address and the browser/device user-agent string) to prevent abuse, under Google's own privacy policy. This happens at the service-provider level; the app itself does not read or store this information.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Android device backup: Depending on your device settings, Android may back up the app's local data (including local saves and sign-in state) to your own Google Drive. This is an Android system feature that you control in your device's Backup settings.

3. Data sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, analytics companies, or any other third party.
  • The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK. - The app's only outbound network connection is to Google Firebase. - The only exception is Google Firebase, described in Section 2, which processes your data solely to provide sign-in and cloud-save services.

4. How long we keep data

  • Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the app, clear the app's data, or clear the site data in your browser. Signing out does not delete local saves and backups. - Cloud saves and your account are kept until you ask us to delete them (see Section 5). - Passwords and Google sign-in credentials are not kept by the app. - Play-session markers are replaced on every launch; an old one expires 120 seconds after it stops renewing.

5. Your rights: viewing, deleting your account and saves

View: After signing in, the Account panel shows the email and account ID currently in use. Sign out: Tap "Sign out" in the Account panel. Sign-out first backs up your progress locally and syncs it to the cloud, then ends the session. Delete your account and cloud saves: The app does not yet offer an in-app account deletion feature. To delete your account, cloud saves, or any data tied to your account, email johnwu0626@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with (or include the account ID shown in the Account panel). We will complete the deletion and reply within 30 days. Deleted cloud saves cannot be recovered.

Delete local data: Uninstall the app, or clear this site's data in your browser.

6. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data without consent, contact us using Section 9 and we will delete it.

7. Security in transit and at rest

  • All connections between the app and Google Firebase are encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. - The Android app does not allow unencrypted cleartext network traffic. - Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; the app never stores or logs them. - Cloud saves can only be accessed by the account owner.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top and publish the latest version at https://johnwu0626.github.io/crusader-coin-pusher-demo/privacy/. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will notify you in the app or at the email you signed in with.
